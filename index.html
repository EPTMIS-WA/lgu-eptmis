System scope

Built for Employee Progress Tracking and Review

01

Task Accountability

Track assignments, assignees, files, progress, approvals, rejections, and reassignment history.

02

Role-Scoped Workflows

Separate dashboards and permissions for Admin, Mayor, Department Head, HRMO, and Employee roles.

03

IPCR Management

Support rating periods, function rows, target commitments, accomplishment reviews, signoffs, and reports.

04

Local-First Operation

Keep daily office work running on a local Django server and SQLite, with optional cloud sync.
